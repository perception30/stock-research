### Position Sizing — Risk-First, Not Capital-First

The right way to size a tight-risk basket is to let the **structural stop determine position size**, capping **total portfolio risk at ~1%** (≈0.2% per name across five). A crucial consequence: at a 1% aggregate cap with sane stops, you typically deploy **well under half your capital** — chasing "full deployment" would force ~1% stops that sit *inside* daily noise and get whipsawed. **Keep the unused capital as dry powder.** Suggested relative weights (of the short-term sleeve actually deployed):
